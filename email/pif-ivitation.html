We are delighted to invite you to the first edition of the PIF Legal Forum, taking place on Tuesday October 22nd, 2024 at The Fairmont Hotel in Riyadh. The PIF Legal Forum seeks to act as a link between PIF, its Portfolio Companies' legal teams, External Counsel, and Regulators in Saudi Arabia. This inaugural event promises to be a platform for meaningful connections and valuable knowledge exchange.

Oct 22nd, 2024
9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Join us
For insightful discussions, networking opportunities, and engaging sessions focused on key legal trends, developments and challenges.
REGISTER TO ATTEND
The one-day event will involve both plenary and breakout sessions, and aims to:
1- Increase coordination, standardization, information sharing, and consistency amongst key stakeholders of the PIF and broader Saudi Arabian legal ecosystem, and improve efficiency of process, development of talent and opportunities across Portfolio Companies' Legal and Compliance Departments, External Counsel, Service Providers, and Regulators.
2- Provide valuable networking opportunities by fostering connections with PIF’s Legal Division, Portfolio Companies Legal Departments, External Counsel, and Regulators.
3- Showcase the capabilities of our external counsel network and strengthen the relationships between these experts, PIF, and our Portfolio Companies.
4- Offer insights into the latest legal and sectoral developments impacting both PIF and our Portfolio Companies, within Saudi Arabia and internationally.
